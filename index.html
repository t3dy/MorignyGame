MORIGNY

Liber Florum Celestis Doctrine — a monastic life & practice simulator

A simulation of the lived experience of John of Morigny (fl. c. 1300–1323), grounded in Claire Fanger's scholarship: the Rule and the Work sharing one body, one clock, and one attention — kept hour by hour toward a condemnation history has already scheduled.
