## 0. The three general paths (WORLD_DESIGN §4)

Writing that varies by disposition axis is tagged:

- **[O] The Obedient** — refuses the Work's risks; the quiet life.
- **[H] The Historical John** — audacious within obedience; the center,
  always the default and the most fully written.
- **[X] The Radical** — John's premises pushed past the record; always
  *priced*, always reachable only through the marked departure.

House rule: [O] and [X] get the same writing care as [H] — no strawmen
at the poles. Fixed history: nothing in [O] or [H] averts 1323; [X]
leaves the record only through the departure annotation.

## 8. Standing risks for the writing program

1. **Register drift under volume.** 247 records is enough to erode the
   three-hands discipline. Mitigation: `WRITING_TEMPLATES.md` carries a
   register checklist per type; coverage tests enforce envelopes but
   cannot enforce voice — spot-read a random 10% each window against
   STYLE_GUIDE.
2. **The [X] pole starving.** Radical content is the smallest column and
   the easiest to defer forever. Each v2 city ships with at least one
   priced [X] beat or the axis goes dead between Étampes and the bar.
3. **Fanger-gated items.** R-2/R-10/R-11 gate the best 3d writing. If
   the reading window slips again, ship 3d with `verify` hedges rather
   than blocking — the apparatus saying "pending the edition" is itself
   on-voice.
4. **Struggle-adjacent copy distractions.** The flesh-kind items in the
   copy pool must pass the same register check as the night material;
   they are the likeliest place for a violation to slip in via haste.
